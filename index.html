About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Hatter Department of Marine Technologies, Leon H. Charney School of Marine Sciences, University of Haifa, Israel, working with Prof. Itzik Klein. My work sits between inertial navigation, computational mathematics and machine learning for dynamical systems.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at the National Institute of Technology Rourkela, India, under Prof. Snehashish Chakraverty. Thesis: Advanced machine learning frameworks for real-world dynamical problems.
